From landing to civilization

Every colony follows the same five stages. The simulator above walks you through each one.

  1. Select landing site

    Weigh terrain, ice, sunlight and radiation across real Martian regions.

  2. Design colony

    Lay out habitats, power and life support by hand or in plain language.

  3. Deploy AI engineers

    Rovers that spot shortfalls, build fixes and repair failures on their own.

  4. Simulate survival

    Dust storms, solar flares and equipment failures test every system.

  5. Expand civilization

    Found outposts at new sites and link them into one network.

The $COLONIZE flywheel

$COLONIZE is the utility token for simulation resources. The simulator uses demo credits to show how the loop is meant to work.

  1. Establish colonies

    Design settlements and deploy autonomous engineering systems.

  2. Spend $COLONIZE

    Unlock advanced simulations, AI agents and infrastructure modules such as fission reactors and the terraforming lab.

  3. Expand civilization

    Colonies produce research data and engineering blueprints that feed the leaderboard.

  4. Reward innovation

    A proposed share of platform fees funds token buybacks and rewards for the best-performing colony designs.

Fee percentages, buybacks and rewards are proposals, not commitments. Demo credits in the simulator have no value and are not tokens. Nothing on this page is financial advice.
